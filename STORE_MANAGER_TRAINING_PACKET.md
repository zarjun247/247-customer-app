# STORE_MANAGER_TRAINING_PACKET

Updated: 2026-05-10.

## Training goal

Prepare store managers to run opening/closing, shift handoff, reconciliation, staff access discipline, rider handoff, dead-letter review, refunds, overrides, degraded mode, and incident escalation without overriding pharmacist or software governance gates.

## Modules

1. **Opening/closing execution:** required signoffs, queue review, dead letters, stock spot-checks, emergency owner check.
2. **Shift reconciliation:** payments, cash/manual transactions, refunds, cancellations, overrides, supplier disputes.
3. **Inventory discipline:** batch/expiry/FEFO, high-value/regulated/cold-chain spot-checks, quarantines, discrepancy freeze.
4. **Rider handoff:** accountable delivery task, POD, COD/cash exposure, failed delivery/return reconciliation.
5. **Override governance:** reason requirements, approver, before/after, daily review, prohibited bypasses.
6. **Incident escalation:** severity, commander, emergency freeze, rollback awareness, evidence preservation.
7. **Degraded mode:** provider outage, app outage, dead-letter backlog, manual fallback packet and backfill.
8. **Access/privacy:** named users only, role/store scope, no shared admin, PHI/PII minimization.

## Required drills

- Complete store opening with unresolved overnight dead letters.
- Complete closing with refund variance and supplier invoice dispute.
- Conduct shift handoff with rider/POD issue.
- Review and reject an override lacking reason.
- Escalate negative stock on regulated item and freeze affected batch.
- Execute manual fallback and reconciliation backfill template.
- Participate in emergency rollback/freeze tabletop.

## Acknowledgement fields

Manager name, store, trainer, drill dates, unresolved competency gaps, SOP versions reviewed, acknowledgement that pharmacist gates cannot be overridden, acknowledgement of reconciliation and emergency stop duties, and launch owner signoff when actually completed.
