# CONTROLLED_DRUG_HANDLING_SOP

Updated: 2026-05-10.

## Scope and non-claims

This SOP governs controlled-drug and high-risk regulated-medicine handling inside the 24/7 Pharmacy OS operational model. It does not assert that any store is licensed or legally approved to stock or dispense controlled drugs. Store capability must be independently verified and attached as evidence before controlled-drug operations are enabled.

## Fail-closed principles

- No controlled-drug purchase, stock movement, sale, packing, rider handoff, delivery, return, disposal, or adjustment proceeds without pharmacist-controlled review and audit evidence.
- AI/OCR is assistive only and cannot approve, substitute, dose, or release.
- Missing licence/capability evidence, missing pharmacist coverage, incomplete prescription details, stock discrepancy, or unresolved statutory context blocks release.

## Handling workflow

1. **Capability check:** confirm store controlled-drug capability is approved in evidence register; otherwise keep capability frozen.
2. **Prescription verification:** pharmacist validates prescription details and identity; uncertain cases are rejected or escalated.
3. **Stock verification:** pharmacist/manager jointly verify batch, expiry, quantity, storage, and ledger status before picking.
4. **Register/statutory record:** complete required controlled-drug/H1 records according to reviewed local process; missing fields block release.
5. **Packing:** segregate from ordinary orders; pharmacist or delegated trained staff records pack confirmation.
6. **Rider handoff:** rider receives sealed package with non-PHI minimal label and delivery task; no substitution or partial delivery without pharmacist review.
7. **Failed delivery/return:** return directly to store owner; reconcile package, batch, and register before restocking/quarantine.
8. **Discrepancy:** emergency freeze affected item/batch/store capability and escalate to pharmacist-in-charge, store manager, and incident commander.

## Escalation triggers

- Suspected forged/altered prescription.
- Early refill or unusual quantity.
- Missing/expired prescription details.
- Batch count mismatch or broken seal.
- Store capability/licence uncertainty.
- Duplicate H1/statutory record attempt.
- Rider loss, failed return, or POD/customer mismatch.

## Required records

Order ID, prescription reference, pharmacist ID, product/batch/expiry, quantity, statutory/register reference where applicable, stock movement ID, packer, rider handoff, delivery/POD or return, discrepancy/incident ID, and reconciliation reviewer.
