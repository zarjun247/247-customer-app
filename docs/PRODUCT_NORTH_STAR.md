# Product North Star — 24/7 Pharmacy

This platform is **not merely a pharmacy app**.

It is **residential medication continuity infrastructure** designed for deterministic, hyperlocal execution in dense urban residential clusters.

## Business Model
- Licensed in-building or building-adjacent pharmacy operations.
- Micro-radius fulfilment in dense premium residential clusters.
- Building-first routing and high-density node economics.

## Strategic Moat
- Density + in-building access.
- SLA execution reliability.
- Closed-loop therapy continuity.
- Compliance-grade auditability.

## Product Principles
- Optimize for deterministic local execution, not generic e-commerce logistics.
- Preserve operational truth across clinical, inventory, fulfilment, and accounting surfaces.
- Medivision can be used as a Phase 1 bridge/source, but native 24/7 OS is the long-term source of truth.
