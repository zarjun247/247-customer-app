# PHARMACIST_TRAINING_PACKET

Updated: 2026-05-10.

## Training goal

Prepare pharmacists to operate the platform safely without weakening pharmacist-gated dispensing, H/H1/X controls, AI boundaries, stock truth, reconciliation truth, or PHI/PII protections. Completion requires observed practice and signed acknowledgement; this packet alone is not signoff.

## Modules

1. **System boundaries:** AI is assistive only; pharmacist owns prescription approval/rejection, regulated release, substitution decisions, and clinical judgement.
2. **Prescription intake/validation:** identity, legibility, prescriber/date/quantity/directions, repeats, rejected prescription paths.
3. **H/H1/X and controlled-drug handling:** fail-closed release, statutory/context records, capability uncertainty, duplicate/suspicious prescriptions.
4. **Stock truth:** batch/expiry/FEFO, quarantines, discrepancies, negative stock, manual fallback count discipline.
5. **Degraded mode:** OCR down, provider down, queue backlog, app outage, manual fallback and later reconciliation.
6. **Escalation:** pharmacist stop-the-line authority, incident commander communication, legal/compliance escalation.
7. **Privacy:** minimum necessary PHI/PII use, redaction expectations, no public-channel leakage.

## Required drills

- Approve valid prescription order with batch/expiry review.
- Reject illegible prescription and send resubmission path.
- Hold H1 order for missing statutory context.
- Freeze a batch after stock discrepancy and reopen only after reconciliation owner action.
- Process OCR outage manually without AI decision authority.
- Trigger emergency stop for suspected forged prescription.
- Shift handoff with unresolved regulated queue.

## Acknowledgement fields

Pharmacist name, registration reference, store, trainer, drill dates, failed/repeated drills, SOP version, acknowledgement that AI cannot approve/release/substitute/dose, acknowledgement of stop-the-line authority, and pharmacist-in-charge signoff when actually completed.
